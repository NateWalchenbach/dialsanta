Su waye mu

Dial Santa ("mu") manhajar nishaɗi ce da iyaye ke jagoranta: babba yana shirya komai kuma yana sayen mintuna, yaro kuma yana amfani da ita tare da iyaye ko mai kula da shi. Wannan manufa ta shafi manhajar Dial Santa ta iOS da wannan gidan yanar gizo.

Abin da muke tattarawa

  • Adireshin imel na iyaye. Wannan shi ne asusunka. Muna amfani da shi don shigar da kai (lambar amfani sau ɗaya, babu kalmar sirri), haɗa mintunan da ka saya, da tura rasidin sayayya. Shiga jerin jiran ƙaddamarwar yanar gizo zaɓi ne daban, ba dole ba.
  • Bayanan keɓance kiran da ka bayar. Sunan farko na yaro, da shekaru, birni, jerin buri ko kyawawan ayyuka idan an so — duk abin da iyaye suka zaɓi shigarwa. Ana amfani da waɗannan don Santa ya gaishe da ɗanka kuma ya yi tattaunawa ta gaske.
  • Bayanan sayayya. Wane kunshin mintuna ka saya da yaushe. Apple ke sarrafa biyan kuɗi na cikin manhajar, Stripe kuma na yanar gizo — ba mu taɓa gani ko adana lambobin katin biyan kuɗi.
  • Bayanan kira na asali. Lokacin da kira ya faru da tsawonsa, domin mu ƙidaya mintuna daidai kuma mu mayar da na kiran da ya gaza.

Jerin jiran ƙaddamarwa

Jerin jiran yanar gizo na manya ne. Idan ka shiga, muna adana adireshin imel ɗinka, ranar rajista da yardar da ka bayar don mu tura maka imel guda ɗaya lokacin ƙaddamar da Dial Santa. Shiga ba ya ƙirƙirar asusun manhaja ko sayen wani abu. Ba ma ƙara imel na asusun manhaja zuwa jerin jira ta atomatik ko amfani da jerin don tallan da bai shafi wannan ba.

Ana adana adiresoshin jerin jira a rumbun bayananmu na Railway har sai mun tura sanarwar ƙaddamarwa ko ka nemi mu cire naka. Don fita daga jerin kafin ƙaddamarwa, tura imel zuwa hohoho@dialsanta.app. Goge asusun manhaja yana cire adireshin jerin jira da ya dace da shi. Muna adana sigar adireshin IP na rajista da aka sarrafa da maɓalli ba za a iya karantawa ba na ɗan lokaci don takaita spam; tana ƙarewa bayan awa ɗaya.

Harshen gidan yanar gizo

Za ka iya zaɓar harshen gidan yanar gizo ta mai zaɓar harshe. Ana adana zaɓinka a cikin burauzarka kuma a haɗa shi cikin hanyoyin haɗi domin harshen ya kasance ɗaya yayin bincike. Ba ma neman wurin da kake.

Website analytics — updated October 13, 2026 (English)

This section replaces the earlier website-analytics description. It applies to public marketing pages in every website language. App analytics and advertising measurement have separate controls below.

Basic visitor measurement: Vercel

Basic Vercel Web Analytics starts automatically on eligible public pages. It measures page views, approximate visitors, referring sites, country/region/city estimates, browser and device information, and App Store button clicks. Vercel derives a visitor hash from network information and discards it within 24 hours; it is not a permanent identity. We remove URL queries and fragments from events. This measurement sets no cookies and does not record website sessions. Selecting Reject optional, saving Analytics off in Cookie preferences, or selecting Turn all analytics off, or sending Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, stops future website analytics. Counts are estimates and may exclude blocked visits.

Detailed website analytics: PostHog

PostHog (US hosting) starts only after you select Allow cookies, save Analytics on in Cookie preferences, or select Allow detailed analytics. It measures page views and exits, referring domains, device/browser/operating-system information, approximate location derived from network information, navigation and App Store clicks, scroll depth, active time, video engagement, and performance. A random browser identifier and session identifier connect activity across pages and repeat visits using browser local storage. They are not linked to your Dial Santa app account. The PostHog project is configured to discard raw IP addresses.

Detailed analytics also includes click and scroll heatmaps and masked session replay of the public website. Replay reconstructs page layout and interactions; it does not record your camera, microphone or calls. Inputs and editable or designated sensitive text are masked; public marketing copy remains visible. Forms, editable areas, embedded frames, canvas, video/audio content and designated sensitive elements are blocked. Network request bodies, headers and console logs are not recorded. New replays are retained for 30 days. Checkout, confirmation and private routes are excluded. We strip query strings and fragments and do not send names, email addresses, child profiles, form entries or call content.

Earlier permission for basic counts does not authorize detailed analytics or replay: we ask again. You can change your choice using Cookie preferences in the website footer. Turning analytics off stops future collection and clears PostHog's local tracking persistence; it does not erase data already received. Browser privacy signals take priority. Anonymous website measurements are separate from app analytics, purchases and advertising. For questions or deletion requests, contact hohoho@dialsanta.app. Vercel privacy details · PostHog privacy details.

Nazarin manhaja na zaɓi

A sigogin manhajar da ke da “Taimaka inganta Dial Santa”, wannan zaɓin dabam a kashe yake tun farko. Idan iyaye suka kunna, API ɗinmu yana tura wa PostHog (mai masaukin bayanai a Amurka) wasu matakan manhaja, sakamakon tabbatarwa da fara amfani, harshe, jarumin da aka zaɓa, nau'in tushen kawo ziyara, sakamakon haɗuwar kira, zaɓin ra'ayi na zaɓi, da kuɗaɗe, kayayyaki da nau'ukan kuɗin saye ko maida kuɗi da aka tabbatar. Alamomin bazuwar manhaja da asusun iyaye suna haɗa waɗannan abubuwa tsakanin zaman amfani da na'urorin da aka shiga. Waɗannan alamomi na sunan aro ne, ba marasa suna gaba ɗaya ba: sabis ɗinmu na iya haɗa ID na iyaye da asusun. Auna yanar gizo ba ya haɗe da shi. Ba ma tura sunaye, adiresoshin imel, bayanan yara, bayanan fom, sautin kira, rubutun tattaunawa, bidiyo, rikodi ko alamomin talla zuwa PostHog. Babu sake kallon zaman mai amfani ko ɗaukar hulɗa ta atomatik. An kashe ƙarin bayanan wuri; abubuwa suna zuwa PostHog ta sabarmu.

Kashe Taimaka inganta Dial Santa a Iyaye don dakatar da abubuwan manhaja na gaba daga wannan na'ura. Zaɓin asusu yana isa sabarmu idan na'urar ta sake haɗuwa; kashewa ba ya goge abubuwan da aka riga aka karɓa. Fita yana share jerin nazari na na'urar kuma yana canja ID ɗin bazuwar na'urar. Goge asusu yana sanya cire mutumin PostHog da abubuwan da ke haɗe a jerin aiki kuma yana nuna sharewa tana jira har sai an tabbatar. Abubuwan da ba su taɓa haɗuwa da asusu ba na iya kasancewa; tuntubi taimako don buƙatun bayanai. Ana riƙe bayanan nazari don rahoton samfur har sai an goge ko wa'adin adanawa mai dacewa ya ƙare. Wannan zaɓi ba ya shafar kira, sayayya ko samun damar Ayyukan nishaɗi.

Website advertising cookies — updated October 13, 2026 (English)

With your permission, this website uses the Meta Pixel to measure whether our Facebook and Instagram ads bring parents to dialsanta.app and show Dial Santa ads to people who have visited. Meta receives pages viewed, App Store button taps, browser and device information, your IP address and Meta cookies (such as _fbp and _fbc) that can last up to 90 days; Meta may link this with your Meta account under its privacy policy. We do not send names, email addresses, child profiles, app accounts, form entries or call content. Automatic advanced matching, automatic event collection and automatic configuration are off; Limited Data Use is on where it applies.

Advertising cookies stay off everywhere until you choose Allow cookies or enable Advertising cookies and save your preferences. Existing explicit permission remains valid. Reject optional turns off advertising and website analytics. Reopen Cookie preferences in the footer to change your choice; the separate Advertising measurement control also works. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track take priority. Turning advertising off stops future sharing and clears the website's _fbp and _fbc cookies, but cannot delete cookies on Meta's domains or data already received by Meta. The Pixel does not run on checkout or confirmation pages or inside the app. Essential browser storage remembers your language and privacy choices.

Abin da ke faruwa yayin kira

Tsarin amincewar asusu da aka bayyana a nan ya shafi sigar 1.0.2 (build 40) da waɗanda suka biyo baya. TestFlight build 39 yana da sarrafa izinin AI na musamman da ikon janye yarda a Iyaye; waɗannan ba sa cikin build 40.

Kira tattaunawa ne kai tsaye da jarumin AI. Shafin imel na iyaye yana nuna rubutu mai hanyoyin haɗi a ƙasa: “Ta ci gaba, ka tabbatar ka karanta kuma ka amince da manufar sirrinmu, sharuɗɗanmu da EULA.” Zaɓin Ci gaba yana shirya wannan yarda; ana rubuta ta bayan an tabbatar da adireshin imel ɗin nan. Masu amfani da suka riga suka shiga amma ba su da yarda ta yanzu suna ganin Ci gaba da Ba yanzu ba tare da wannan rubutun ƙasa kafin kira; ba a buƙatar sake tabbatar da imel don wannan yarda kaɗai. An bayyana raba bayanan AI da sunayen masu samar da sabis a wannan manufa, ba tare da wani kwamitin raba AI na dabam a manhajar ba. Izinin muryar iOS daban ne. Ana sarrafa muryar ɗanka a lokacin nan take don jarumin ya ji kuma ya amsa. Hoton ganin kanka na kamara yana nan a na'urarka; ba a tura shi ga masu samar da AI ko sabobinmu. Rikodin na'ura na zaɓi yana haɗa wannan hoton.

  • Rikodi a kashe yake tun farko. Rikodin allo da murya na zaɓi suna nan a na'urarka. An kashe rikodin murya na ElevenLabs; an saita wakilansa su goge sautin tattaunawa da rubutunta ba tare da riƙe su na ko kwana ɗaya ba. Wannan ba Enterprise Zero Retention Mode na ElevenLabs ba ne. LiveAvatar na iya adana rubutun tattaunawa, bayanan zaman kira da mahallin keɓancewa. Goge asusu yana neman cire tattaunawar ElevenLabs da mahallin/abubuwan LiveAvatar kuma yana tabbatar rubutun LiveAvatar babu komai. Yana dakatar da zaman LemonSlice; ba ya tabbatar da goge tsoffin bayanan log ko kafofin watsa labarai na LemonSlice. Bayanan fasaha, tsaro, lissafin kuɗi da waɗanda doka ta wajabta na masu samar da sabis na iya samun wa'adin adanawa daban.
  • Fara kira yana tura wa Dial Santa sunan farko na yaro, shekaru da birni idan an shigar, jerin buri da kyawawan ayyuka, da harshen da aka zaɓa. Sabar bayan fagenmu tana tura bayanan da suka dace ga masu samar da sabis da ke ƙasa don keɓance tattaunawar. Abin da aka faɗa, rubutun tattaunawa da amsoshin da aka ƙirƙira na iya ɗaukar bayanan sirri. Ka raba abin da kake son jarumin ya sani kawai.
  • An tsara Santa kada ya nemi bayanan sirri daga yaro kuma kada ya yi alkawarin takamaiman kyaututtuka.

Rikodin kira na zaɓi

A kiran da ke tallafawa, Naɗa yana ɗaukar allon kira, har da hoton ganin kanka na kamara, da bangarorin tattaunawar biyu. Maɓallin rikodi da ke bayyane yana ba ka damar dakatar da shi. Kashe muryar makirufo yana kashe muryarka a rikodin ma. Rikodi yana tsayawa idan kira ya ƙare ko manhajar ta koma bayan fage.

Rikodi fayiloli ne na ɗan lokaci a na'urarka. Dial Santa ba ya ɗora su zuwa sabobinmu ko amfani da su don talla. Bayan kira, za ka iya duba su, goge su ko zaɓar Adana a Photos. Adanawa yana neman izini don ƙara bidiyo zuwa ɗakin hotunanka kawai, ba karanta hotunan da kake da su ba. Idan adanawa ya gaza ko an hana izini, rikodin yana nan a shafin ƙarshen kira don ka sake gwadawa.

Adana rikodi a Photos idan kana son riƙe shi. Rikodin da ba a adana ba ana cire shi idan ka jefar da shi, ka bar shafin duba kira bayan tabbatar da gogewa, ko ka sake kunna manhajar. Bidiyon da aka adana a Photos yana ƙarƙashin saitunan Photos da iCloud ɗinka. Goge asusun Dial Santa ba ya goge bidiyon da ka adana a Photos; ka goge su da kanka a Photos.

Masu samar da sabis

Waɗannan kamfanoni suna taimakawa manhajar. Masu samar da kira suna karɓar bayanan nan don dalilan da aka bayyana:

  • ElevenLabs yana sarrafa sautin makirufo, rubutun tattaunawa da keɓancewa don ƙirƙirar amsoshi da muryoyin Santa, Mrs. Claus da Comet. Sarrafa samfurin harshensa yana amfani da OpenAI tare da rubutun tattaunawa da bayanan mutum, har da lokacin da ake buƙatar wani samfurin madadi. HeyGen (LiveAvatar) yana karɓar muryar da aka ƙirƙira don motsa Santa da Mrs. Claus; wani yanayin kira na madadi kuma yana sarrafa sautin makirufo, rubutun tattaunawa da keɓancewa a can. LemonSlice (Infinity AI, Inc.) yana karɓar muryar da aka ƙirƙira don motsa Comet. Muryar da aka ƙirƙira na iya maimaita bayanan da aka raba yayin kira.
  • LiveKit yana jigilar kiran Santa da Mrs. Claus; Daily yana jigilar kiran Comet. Suna jigilar sautin makirufo da aka ɓoye, sauti/bidiyon jarumi, rubutun allo, abubuwan rubutun tattaunawa da alamomin zaman kira. Wannan bai haɗa da hoton ganin kanka na kamara ba. Masu samar da sabis kuma suna sarrafa bayanan haɗuwa da gudanar da sabis.
  • Apple, Stripe da RevenueCat — Apple da Stripe suna sarrafa sayayya da rasidi ta sabis ɗinsu na biyan kuɗi. RevenueCat yana karɓar alamar asusu da ba ta bayyana mai shi ba da bayanan cinikin Apple don tabbatarwa da bayar da rahoton sayayyar manhaja. Ba ma tura wa RevenueCat bayanan yara, rikodin murya ko abin da ke cikin kira. Idan ka goge asusunka, muna neman goge bayanan abokin ciniki na RevenueCat da ke da alaƙa.
  • Railway — yana ɗaukar bakuncin sabobinmu da rumbun bayanai (a Amurka).
  • Meta — yana auna waɗanne tallace-tallacen Dial Santa suka sa an girka manhajar. Sau ɗaya, bayan iyaye sun yi rajista sun tabbatar da imel, SDK na Meta a manhajar yana tura abin girkawa da buɗe manhaja tare da bayanan na'ura na asali (kamar nau'in na'ura, sigar iOS, harshe, yankin lokaci da ID marar suna na musamman ga manhajar). SDK ba ya sake farawa idan an buɗe manhajar daga baya. Meta yana karɓar alamar tallar na'urar nan ne kawai idan ka ba da izinin bibiyar lokacin da iOS ya tambaya. An kashe rubuta abubuwa ta atomatik, haɗa bayanai na ci gaba ta atomatik da rahoton faɗuwar Meta, kuma an kunna Limited Data Use na Meta. Ba ma tura bayanan yara, sunaye, shekaru, murya, abin da ke cikin kira, adiresoshin imel, bayanan asusu ko sayayya zuwa Meta. Bayanai da Meta ya riga ya karɓa suna ƙarƙashin manufofin Meta kansa.

Kariyar masu samar da sabis ta haɗa da jigilar da aka ɓoye da alkawurran tsaro, samun dama da haƙƙoƙi a sharuɗɗansu masu dacewa. Sharuɗɗan sarrafa bayanai na HeyGen, ElevenLabs, LiveKit da Daily suna magana kan umarnin sarrafawa, sirri, tsaro da ƙananan masu sarrafawa. Iyakar aikinsu da dokokin adanawarsu sun bambanta. Ba mu taɓa sayar da bayanan sirri.

Mun kashe zaɓin horar da samfuri a wurin aikin ElevenLabs don sababbin bayanan da ake tura wa; wannan ba ya goge tsoffin bayanai. Abubuwan amfani da masu samar da sabis suka yarda da su ma sun bambanta. Manufar HeyGen tana ba da damar amfani da wasu bayanan asusun da ba na Enterprise ba don horar da samfuri sai idan an yi amfani da zaɓin ƙin shiga da ya dace. Sharuɗɗan LemonSlice suna yarda da inganta samfur da horar da samfuri; manufar sirrinsa tana bayyana kariya da adanawa. Waɗannan ba alkawurran gama-gari ba ne cewa ba za a yi horarwa ko adana bayanai ba. Tuntube mu don taimako da buƙatun samun dama ko goge bayanan masu samar da sabis.

Sirrin yara

An gina Dial Santa don iyaye su gudanar. Babba ke yin asusu, sayayya da saiti; kariyar iyaye tana kare duk abin da ya shafi kuɗi, sarrafa sirri ko fita daga manhajar. Muna tattara daga yara abin da iyaye suka shigar a madadinsu kawai (kamar sunan farko), tare da sarrafa murya kai tsaye da aka bayyana a sama — bayan iyaye sun amince da sharuɗɗan asusu da manufar sirri da aka haɗa kamar yadda aka bayyana. Dial Santa ba ya nuna tallan ɓangare na uku. Iyaye suna amincewa da auna talla a rajista, yana faruwa sau ɗaya bayan an tabbatar da imel na iyaye, za a iya kashe shi a Iyaye, kuma ba ya karɓar bayanan yara ko kira. Muna bin ƙa'idodin COPPA: ƙarancin tattarawa, yardar iyaye, babu tallan halayya ga yara, da ikon iyaye kan gogewa.

Zaɓuɓɓukanka da haƙƙoƙinka

  • Goge asusunka: buɗe Iyaye → Asusu → Goge asusu, tabbatar da imel sannan ka amince. Muna cire imel na asusunka, ragowar mintuna da bayanan kira daga rumbun bayananmu mai aiki, kuma mu share bayanan yaro da tarihinsa a na'urar nan. Sauran na'urorin da aka shiga suna fita kuma suna share bayanan asusu na cikin su idan suka sake tuntuɓar sabarmu. Share bayanan masu samar da kira yana aiki ta atomatik a bayan fage kuma yana sake gwadawa idan an samu matsala ta ɗan lokaci. Yawanci sharewa tana ɗaukar 'yan mintuna; katsewar sabis na ɗan lokaci na iya tsawaita. Duba Iyaye → Asusu don tabbatar da kammalawa, ko tuntubi taimako.
  • Fita: buɗe Iyaye → Asusu → Fita. Mintunan da ka saya suna nan a imel ɗinka, amma ana cire bayanan yaro da tarihin na'urar daga wannan na'ura.
  • Duba bayananka: tuntubi taimako don neman damar dubawa.
  • Dakatar da raba kira na gaba: ƙare duk kiran da ke gudana kuma kada ka fara wani kiran AI. Za ka iya fita a Iyaye → Asusu; hakan yana hana sababbin kiran kan layi a na'urar har sai ka sake shiga. Ayyukan nishaɗi marasa intanet suna nan. Build 40 ba shi da maɓallin raba AI a Iyaye. Dakatar da kira ko fita ba ya goge bayanan da aka riga aka sarrafa. Yi amfani da goge asusu ko tuntubi taimako don neman gogewa.
  • Auna talla: buɗe Iyaye → Sirri da tsaro → Kashe auna talla. Daga nan Dial Santa ba ya raba wani abu da Meta daga na'urar nan, ko da ka sake shiga, kuma kana nan a shiga. Haka kuma za ka iya ƙin tambayar bibiyar Apple ko kashe Saituna → Sirri da Tsaro → Bibiyar don hana alamar talla. Wasu dokokin sirri na jihohin Amurka na iya ɗaukar raba bayanan na'ura don auna talla a matsayin "raba" bayanan sirri; sarrafawar Iyaye ita ce hanyar ƙin wannan a na'urarka, kuma za ka iya turo mana imel.
  • Goge asusu yana cire mintunan da ba a yi amfani da su ba kuma ba ya mayar da kuɗi ta atomatik. Tuntubi taimako don taimakon maida kuɗi.

Tsaro da adanawa

Ana ɓoye duk zirga-zirgar bayanai yayin jigila (HTTPS/TLS). Ana adana lambobin shiga a matsayin hashes na hanya ɗaya kawai kuma suna ƙarewa cikin mintuna 10. Muna riƙe bayanan asusu da sayayya yayin da asusunka yake nan da kuma kamar yadda haraji da lissafi ke buƙata; bayanan ƙidayar kira ana adana su kawai muddin ake buƙata don daidaiton lissafin kuɗi. Bayan goge asusu, muna riƙe taƙaitattun bayanan asusu da aka sarrafa da maɓalli da alamomin biyan kuɗi don hana tsoffin bayanan shiga ko sanarwar biyan kuɗi mai maimaituwa dawo da asusun da aka goge. Masu sarrafa biyan kuɗi na iya riƙe bayanan ciniki. Ayyukan goge bayanan masu samar da sabis na ɗan lokaci suna riƙe alamomin da ake buƙata don kammala gogewa a wurinsu; ana cire su bayan an gama. Sharewar masu samar da sabis tana bin iyakokin da aka bayyana a sama; bayanan fasaha, tsaro ko doka na dabam na iya kasancewa bisa sharuɗɗansu.
