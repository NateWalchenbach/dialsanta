1. Tīkina Dial Santa

Whakamahia te whārangi mana Dial Santa i te App Store. He koreutu te tiki. Me hoko meneti ngā waea i roto i te taupānga mā Apple. Kāore te tiki anake e tīmata ohaurunga.

2. Whakaritea te pūkete mātua

Whāia ngā hipanga manatoko īmēra i te taupānga, pānuihia ngā tikanga me te kaupapahere tūmataitinga whai hononga. Mā te matua te whakarite. Ka tiaki te ārai mātua i ngā hoko me ngā tautuhinga, engari kāore e whakakapi i te tirotiro pakeke i te kōrerorero.

3. Tāpirihia ētahi taipitopito whaiaro

Whakauruhia te ingoa tuatahi. Ka taea hoki he wawata, he mahi pai rānei, pēnei i te āwhina tuakana, teina rānei, te ako mea hou rānei. Kāore e hiahiatia he tuhinga whakaari. Homai anake ngā taipitopito e pai ana kia tukatukatia mō te waea; kaua he wāhitau kāinga, ingoa kura, mōhiohio whānau tairongo rānei.

4. Kōwhiria ngā meneti me te kiripuaki

Ko ngā mōkihi o Amerika he 5 meneti mō US$2.99, 12 mō US$5.99, 30 mō US$15.99. Ka rerekē pea ngā utu App Store ā-rohe. Kāore ngā meneti e pau i te hipanga o te wā, kāore he ohaurunga. Kua whakaurua a Hana Kōkō; me wewete motuhake tana Wahine me Comet, he US$0.99 ia tangata ki Amerika, me hoko meneti waea hoki.

Ka roa ngā waea Hana Kōkō me tana Wahine ki te ono meneti ia waea, a Comet ki te toru. Ka poto ake pea nā tō toenga. Ka inea ngā meneti ia hēkona tūhono, nō reira ka noho te wā kāore i whakamahia ki tō pūkete.

Kimihia he wāhi mārie, whakapikihia te oro, whakaaetia te hopuoro ina tonoa. Kōwhiria te kiripuaki, tīmatahia te waea. Ka rongo, ka whakautu a Hana Kōkō ki te reo. Mā te tirohanga kāmera e kite ai tō whānau i a rātou anō ki te pūrere; kāore e tukuna ki ō mātou tūmau, ki ngā kaiwhakarato AI rānei.

Tīmata mā te mihi māmā. Ki te noho puku te tamaiti, āwhinatia mā “Kei te whakaaro mātou me pēhea ngā renitia e whakarite ai mō Kirihimete.” Whāia te tere o te tamaiti, whakamutua te waea ina hiahia.
