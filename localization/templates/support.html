Common questions

I never got my 6-digit sign-in code

Check your spam or junk folder first — the email comes from Dial Santa. You can request a new code after 30 seconds from the sign-in screen. Still nothing after a couple of tries? Email us with the address you used and we'll get you in.

I bought minutes but they're not showing in the app

Minutes usually land within a few seconds of checkout. If they haven't:

  • Make sure the parent email attached to your purchase is the same email you signed into the app with — minutes attach to the email.
  • Close and reopen the app; your balance refreshes automatically.
  • Still missing? Email us with your purchase receipt and we'll credit the minutes manually within hours.
Santa didn't answer, or the call dropped

If a call fails to connect or a character doesn't pick up, those minutes are returned to your balance automatically — you only pay for conversation that actually happened. If a call dropped mid-conversation and you feel shortchanged, email us and we'll top you back up.

How do refunds work?

For Apple purchases, request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem service. For website purchases paid through Stripe, email us within 30 days about unused minutes. Failed calls return unused time to your minute balance automatically.

Is Santa real? (What my kid sees vs. what's happening)

For your kid: absolutely. For you: it's a live AI performance — a video avatar and voice holding a real conversation, personalized with the details you entered. Replies are generated live. Recording is optional and stays on your device; our call provider can retain text transcripts until you delete your account. The Parents' guide has the full picture.

Delete my account and data

In the app, open Parents → Account → Delete account, verify your email and confirm. You can check call-provider cleanup there too. Deletion removes unused minutes and does not automatically refund purchases. Contact us from your account email if you need help.
