Publisher privacy policy

This policy describes the current internal trial of Dial Santa Publishing / Dial Santa Paperclip Publisher, operated by Nate Walchenbach. It applies to this publishing client, not the separate Dial Santa iPhone app. Published September 30, 2026.

Data and purpose

The client uses YouTube API Services to identify the authorized channel, upload owner-approved videos privately, verify their processing and metadata, and support separately approved public release when provider and local requirements are met. It also retrieves selected reports for registered Dial Santa videos.

Data includes OAuth credentials, channel and video identifiers, titles, descriptions, privacy and processing status, selected video metrics and operation results. Local inputs include media files, source-file hashes, drafts, audience and synthetic-media settings, and approval records. It does not collect information from child viewers or record calls made in the consumer app.

Storage, access and processors

OAuth credentials are held in Paperclip's encrypted secret vault; access tokens are cached in adapter memory. Drafts, media, tool histories, reports and evidence can be stored on the owner's computer and in database backups. Ordinary project files are not represented as separately encrypted by the application.

Google receives authorization requests, approved videos, metadata and API calls. The owner and authorized operators can access local records. Merry uses an OpenAI-backed Codex runtime; API results may be included in its context and conversation history. The owner's inspected personal ChatGPT account currently has model improvement enabled. We do not claim a no-training or zero-retention arrangement for this runtime. See OpenAI's privacy policy and Codex deletion guidance.

The publisher does not sell API data, offer it to other channel owners, or embed a YouTube player or advertisements. This static overview has no publisher analytics or login script. Google's sign-in and other external services have their own policies.

Retention and deletion requests

The internal trial currently retains API data in local publishing records, completed Paperclip runs, operational documents and backups. Backup settings use daily, weekly and monthly tiers; they do not by themselves enforce deletion from every copy. OpenAI's published Codex guidance describes deletion within 30 days after a chat is deleted, subject to its stated exceptions.

Operator tools can preview and redact the publishing database and selected completed YouTube-only run records, remove their verified execution files, and prepare cleaned database-backup copies while preserving other projects. They have been tested with synthetic data. No live cleanup has been performed as part of the trial.

Current limitation. These controls do not automatically cover all mixed project documents, later evidence, storage snapshots or model-provider history. A complete seven-day deletion process has not yet been verified. This policy discloses the current implementation; it is not a claim that the client has passed Google's compliance audit.

To request access, correction or deletion of publisher data, email nate.walchenbach@gmail.com with the channel or record concerned. The operator handles requests manually, stops affected processing as appropriate, identifies local and provider copies, and reports the actions taken and any remaining limits. Do not send your password or OAuth tokens.

Local duplicate-upload reservations may be kept after API payloads are removed so an uncertain upload is not accidentally repeated. Local cleanup does not delete a video from YouTube.

Revoke authorization and stop use

You can revoke access in Google Account permissions. The owner can disable the Paperclip connection and remove the live stored credential. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not itself erase existing histories or backups. Request deletion separately using the contact above. An end-to-end disconnect and all-copy deletion test has not been completed.
