Owner approval and content rights

Operators must have the rights to submitted media and provide accurate titles, descriptions, audience classifications and synthetic-media disclosures. The exact video, metadata and requested action require owner approval. A private upload requires authorization; public release requires separate authorization, the applicable Google project audit and completed verification.

The client is currently in a private trial. No public posting schedule is enabled. Neither these terms nor an OAuth connection grants an agent standing permission to publish any content it chooses.

Reliability

The client checks the selected channel and records an upload reservation before making the request. An unknown result must be reconciled before a retry. Preparing a draft is not uploading it. An upload does not guarantee processing, public availability or classification as a Short.

Stopping use

The owner can disable the connection or revoke access through Google. Existing local records and videos on YouTube require separate deletion actions. Use the privacy-policy contact for publisher data requests and YouTube's controls for remote videos unless a specific additional action is authorized.
