Dial Santa, at a glance

Product facts and approved assets for editors, creators and family guides. Last checked October 1, 2026.

One sentence

Dial Santa is an iPhone app that lets families have live, two-way video conversations with AI characters from the North Pole, with parent-led setup and one-time call-minute purchases.

Product facts

Product
Dial Santa
Developer and seller
Natura AI LLC
Platform
iPhone, iOS 17 or later
Availability
Available on the App Store
Price
Free download; calls require purchased minutes. US packs: 5 minutes / US$2.99, 12 / US$5.99, 30 / US$15.99. No subscription. Minutes never expire. Local prices may vary.
Characters
Santa is included. Mrs. Claus and Comet each have a separate US$0.99 US in-app unlock, plus call minutes. Other characters shown as coming soon are not available for calls.
Languages
Santa and Mrs. Claus: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Russian. Comet: English.
Call duration
Up to six minutes per Santa or Mrs. Claus call, or three minutes for Comet, also limited by the available minute balance.

What to describe accurately

The characters are AI performances, not human performers. They respond to voice and parent-provided personalization. The camera is a local self-view; it is not sent to the AI providers or our servers. Calls do not use Apple FaceTime. Parents should set up and supervise the experience. Please refer to the current privacy policy for provider processing and retention, rather than describing calls as “zero retention.”
