Common questions

I never got my 6-digit sign-in code

Check your spam or junk folder first — the email comes from Dial Santa. You can request a new code after 30 seconds from the sign-in screen. Still nothing after a couple of tries? Email us with the address you used and we'll get you in.

I bought minutes but they're not showing in the app

Minutes usually land within a few seconds of checkout. If they haven't:

  • Make sure the email you entered at Stripe checkout is the same email you signed into the app with — minutes attach to the email.
  • Close and reopen the app; your balance refreshes automatically.
  • Still missing? Email us with your Stripe receipt and we'll credit the minutes manually within hours.
Santa didn't answer, or the call dropped

If a call fails to connect or a character doesn't pick up, those minutes are returned to your balance automatically — you only pay for conversation that actually happened. If a call dropped mid-conversation and you feel shortchanged, email us and we'll top you back up.

How do refunds work?

Email us within 30 days of purchase and we'll refund unused minutes to your original payment method, no interrogation. Failed calls are refunded to your minute balance automatically without asking.

Is Santa real? (What my kid sees vs. what's happening)

For your kid: absolutely. For you: it's a live AI performance — a video avatar and voice holding a real conversation, personalized with the details you entered. Nothing is pre-recorded, and calls are never stored. The Parents' guide has the full picture.

Delete my account and data

Email us from your account address with "delete my account" and we'll erase everything — your email, your child's name, and call records — and confirm when it's done. Unused minutes can be refunded before deletion; just say so in the same email.
