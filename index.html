FOR PARENTS

You set the stage.
Santa plays the part.

Everything about the call is in your hands — before, during, and after.

Parent dashboard behind a grown-ups gate

Profiles, wish lists, call history, and settings sit behind a hold-and-answer parental gate.

Private by design

Your kid's voice is processed in real time to make the conversation work — never stored by Dial Santa, never used to train anything, never shared.

Hard limits on every call

Each call caps at six minutes with a gentle wind-down. Leftover minutes never disappear — they stay in your balance for the next call, and you're only charged for connected time.
