A few things
grown-ups ask.

Is it really a live call?+

Yes. The conversation happens live: your child speaks, and Santa responds to what they say. It’s an interactive video call with an AI Santa, rather than a prerecorded personalized message.

Is the app free?+

Dial Santa is free to download. Calls require purchased minutes, starting at US$2.99 for five minutes. There is no subscription.

How long can we talk?+

Santa and Mrs. Claus calls can last up to six minutes; Comet calls up to three. The available balance also sets the limit. Minutes are used by the connected second, and remaining minutes stay in your account.

Is a grown-up part of the experience?+

Yes. Dial Santa is designed for a parent or guardian to set up and supervise. Stay nearby, especially with younger children. You control profiles, purchases, and when the call ends.

Can we use FaceTime to call Santa?+

Dial Santa is a separate iPhone app for live AI video conversations. It does not use Apple FaceTime and does not provide a Santa phone number. A grown-up sets up the app and buys call minutes.
