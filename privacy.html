PRIVACY POLICY

Your family's privacy, plainly.

Effective October 13, 2026 (English analytics update)
The short version: we collect a parent's email address and the small details you choose to share so Santa can personalize the call (a first name, a wish list). Calls happen live — calls are not recorded automatically. On supported calls, you can choose to record the call screen and both voices on your device and save the video to Photos. Dial Santa does not upload these recordings to our servers; provider transcripts and deletion limits are explained below. We do not show third-party ads or sell data. When a parent signs up in the app, Dial Santa tells Meta once that the app was installed and opened, so we can see which of our ads work; this never includes child profiles, voice, calls, email, or purchases. Turn that off, sign out, or permanently delete your account in the app under Parents.

Who we are

Dial Santa ("we," "us") is a parent-directed entertainment app: a grown-up sets everything up and buys minutes, and a child uses it together with their parent or guardian. This policy covers the Dial Santa iOS app and this website.

What we collect

  • Parent email address. This is your account. We use it to sign you in (a one-time code, no password), attach your purchased minutes, and send purchase receipts. Joining the website launch waitlist is a separate, optional choice.
  • Call personalization you provide. A child's first name, and optionally an age, city, wish list, or good deeds — whatever a parent chooses to enter. These exist so Santa can greet your child and hold a real conversation.
  • Purchase records. Which minute pack you bought and when. Payments are handled by Apple for in-app purchases and Stripe for website purchases — we never see or store card numbers.
  • Basic call records. When a call happened and how long it lasted, so we can meter minutes correctly and refund calls that fail.

Launch waitlist

The website waitlist is for adults. If you join, we save your email address, signup date, and the consent you gave so we can send you one email when Dial Santa launches. Joining does not create an app account or purchase anything. We do not add app account emails to the waitlist automatically or use the list for unrelated marketing.

Waitlist addresses are stored in our Railway database until we send the launch announcement or you ask us to remove yours. To leave the waitlist before launch, email hohoho@dialsanta.app. Deleting an app account also removes the matching waitlist address. We briefly store a keyed, unreadable version of the signup IP address to limit spam; it expires after one hour.

Website language

You can choose your website language using the language selector. Your selection is saved locally in your browser and included in links so you can keep the same language as you browse. We do not request your location.

Website analytics — updated October 13, 2026 (English)

This section replaces the earlier website-analytics description. It applies to public marketing pages in every website language. App analytics and advertising measurement have separate controls below.

Basic visitor measurement: Vercel

Basic Vercel Web Analytics starts automatically on eligible public pages. It measures page views, approximate visitors, referring sites, country/region/city estimates, browser and device information, and App Store button clicks. Vercel derives a visitor hash from network information and discards it within 24 hours; it is not a permanent identity. We remove URL queries and fragments from events. This measurement uses no advertising cookies and does not record website sessions. Selecting Decline analytics or Turn all analytics off, or sending Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, stops future website analytics. Counts are estimates and may exclude blocked visits.

Detailed website analytics: PostHog

PostHog (US hosting) starts only after you select Allow detailed analytics. It measures page views and exits, referring domains, device/browser/operating-system information, approximate location derived from network information, navigation and App Store clicks, scroll depth, active time, video engagement, and performance. A random browser identifier and session identifier connect activity across pages and repeat visits using browser local storage. They are not linked to your Dial Santa app account. The PostHog project is configured to discard raw IP addresses.

Detailed analytics also includes click and scroll heatmaps and masked session replay of the public website. Replay reconstructs page layout and interactions; it does not record your camera, microphone or calls. Inputs and editable or designated sensitive text are masked; public marketing copy remains visible. Forms, editable areas, embedded frames, canvas, video/audio content and designated sensitive elements are blocked. Network request bodies, headers and console logs are not recorded. New replays are retained for 30 days. Checkout, confirmation and private routes are excluded. We strip query strings and fragments and do not send names, email addresses, child profiles, form entries or call content.

Earlier permission for basic counts does not authorize detailed analytics or replay: we ask again. You can change your choice in the website footer. Turning analytics off stops future collection and clears PostHog's local tracking persistence; it does not erase data already received. Browser privacy signals take priority. Anonymous website measurements are separate from app analytics, purchases and advertising. For questions or deletion requests, contact hohoho@dialsanta.app. Vercel privacy details · PostHog privacy details.

Optional app analytics

In app versions offering “Help improve Dial Santa”, this separate choice is off by default. If a parent enables it, our API sends PostHog (US hosting) selected app steps, verification and onboarding outcomes, language, selected character, referral category, call connection outcomes, optional feedback choices, and verified purchase or refund amounts, products and currencies. Random app and parent-account identifiers connect these events across sessions and signed-in devices. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not anonymous: our service can associate the parent ID with the account. Website measurement is not linked to it. We do not send names, email addresses, child profiles, form entries, call audio, transcripts, video, recordings or advertising identifiers to PostHog. There is no session replay or automatic interaction capture. Location enrichment is disabled; events reach PostHog through our server.

Turn off Help improve Dial Santa in Parents to stop future app events from that device. The account preference reaches our server when the device reconnects; already received events are not erased by turning it off. Signing out clears the local analytics queue and rotates the device’s random ID. Account deletion queues removal of the linked PostHog person and events and reports cleanup as pending until verified. Events never linked to an account may remain; contact support for data requests. Analytics records are kept for product reporting until deletion or the applicable retention period. This choice does not affect calling, purchases or access to Activities.

Advertising measurement on this website

This website uses the Meta Pixel so we can measure whether our Facebook and Instagram ads bring parents to dialsanta.app and show Dial Santa ads to people who have visited. While it runs, Meta receives the pages you view, which App Store button you tap, browser and device information, your IP address and a Meta cookie that lasts up to 90 days; Meta may link this with your Meta account under Meta’s own privacy policy. We do not send names, email addresses, child profiles, app accounts, form entries or call content. Meta’s automatic advanced matching, automatic event collection and automatic configuration are turned off, and Meta’s Limited Data Use setting is on where it applies. If your browser’s time zone is in the EU, UK or Switzerland, the Pixel stays off until you choose Allow in the banner; elsewhere it runs unless you turn it off. You can change your choice anytime under Advertising measurement in the website footer, and we honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. Turning it off stops future sharing but does not erase information Meta already received. The Pixel does not run on checkout or confirmation pages or inside the app.

What happens during a call

The account-acceptance flow described here applies to version 1.0.2 (build 40) and later. TestFlight build 39 had AI-specific permission controls and a Parents withdrawal control; those controls are not present in build 40.

Calls are live conversations with an AI character. The parent email screen displays a linked footer: “By continuing, you confirm you have read and agree to our privacy policy, terms & conditions, and EULA.” Choosing Continue stages that acceptance; it is recorded after the same email address is verified. Existing signed-in users without a current acceptance receive Continue and Not now with the same linked footer before a call; email reverification is not required solely for that acceptance. AI data sharing and provider names are explained in this policy, without a separate AI-sharing panel in the app. iOS microphone permission is separate. Your child’s voice is processed in real time so the character can hear and respond. The camera self-view stays on your device; it is not sent to the AI providers or our servers. An optional local recording includes that self-view.

  • Recording is off by default. Optional screen-and-voice recordings stay on your device. ElevenLabs voice recording is disabled; its agents are configured to delete conversation audio and transcript data with zero-day retention. This is not ElevenLabs' enterprise Zero Retention Mode. LiveAvatar can retain transcripts, session records and personalized contexts. Account deletion requests removal of ElevenLabs conversations and LiveAvatar contexts/events and verifies the LiveAvatar transcript is empty. It stops LemonSlice sessions; it does not verify deletion of any historical LemonSlice logs or media. Provider technical, security, billing and legally required records may have separate retention.
  • Starting a call sends Dial Santa the child's first name, optional age, city, wish list and good deeds, and selected language. Our backend passes the relevant details to the providers below to personalize the conversation. Spoken words, transcripts and generated replies may also contain personal information. Please share only what you want the character to know.
  • Santa is designed never to ask a child for personal information, and never to promise specific gifts.

Optional call recordings

On supported calls, Record captures the call screen, including the camera self-view, and both sides of the conversation. A visible recording control lets you stop recording. Muting your microphone also mutes your voice in the recording. Recording stops when the call ends or the app goes into the background.

Recordings are temporary files on your device. Dial Santa does not upload them to our servers or use them for advertising. After the call, you can preview, delete, or choose Save to Photos. Saving requests permission only to add a video to your library, not to read your existing photos. If saving fails or permission is denied, the recording remains available on the call's end screen so you can try again.

Save a recording to Photos if you want to keep it. Unsaved recordings are removed when you discard them, leave the call's review screen after confirming deletion, or restart the app. Videos saved to Photos are controlled by your Photos and iCloud settings. Deleting your Dial Santa account does not delete videos you saved to Photos; delete those in Photos yourself.

Service providers

These companies support the app. The call providers receive the following data for the stated purposes:

  • ElevenLabs processes microphone audio, transcripts and personalization to generate replies and voices for Santa, Mrs. Claus and Comet. Its language-model processing uses OpenAI with conversation and profile text, including when an alternative model is needed. HeyGen (LiveAvatar) receives generated speech to animate Santa and Mrs. Claus; a fallback call mode also processes microphone audio, transcripts and personalization there. LemonSlice (Infinity AI, Inc.) receives generated speech to animate Comet. Generated speech can repeat details shared during the call.
  • LiveKit transports Santa and Mrs. Claus calls; Daily transports Comet calls. They carry encrypted microphone audio, character audio/video, captions, transcript events and session identifiers. This does not include your camera self-view. Providers also process connection and service-operation information.
  • Apple, Stripe and RevenueCat — Apple and Stripe process purchases and receipts through their respective payment services. RevenueCat receives an opaque account identifier and Apple transaction information to verify and report in-app purchases. We do not send RevenueCat child profiles, voice recordings or call content. When you delete your account, we request deletion of its RevenueCat customer record.
  • Railway — hosts our servers and database (located in the United States).
  • Meta — measures which Dial Santa ads led to app installs. Once, after a parent signs up and verifies their email, Meta's SDK in the app sends an install and app-open event with basic device information (such as device model, iOS version, language, time zone, and an app-specific anonymous ID). The SDK doesn't start again when the app is opened later. Meta receives this device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. Automatic event logging, automatic advanced matching, and Meta crash reports are turned off, and Meta's Limited Data Use setting is on. We do not send child profiles, names, ages, voice, call content, email addresses, account details, or purchases to Meta. Information Meta has already received is handled under Meta's own policies.

Provider protections include encrypted transmission and the security, access and rights commitments in their applicable terms. The data-processing terms of HeyGen, ElevenLabs, LiveKit and Daily address processing instructions, confidentiality, security and subprocessors. Their scope and retention rules differ. We never sell personal information.

We have turned off ElevenLabs’ workspace model-training preference for new submissions; this does not erase historical data. Provider permitted uses also differ. HeyGen's policy allows some non-enterprise data to be used for model training unless an applicable opt-out applies. LemonSlice's terms permit product improvement and model training; its privacy policy describes its safeguards and retention. These are not universal no-training or zero-retention commitments. Contact us for help with provider data access or deletion requests.

Children's privacy

Dial Santa is built for parents to run. Accounts, purchases, and setup are done by an adult; a parental gate protects anything involving money, privacy controls, or leaving the app. We collect from children only what a parent enters on their behalf (like a first name), plus the real-time voice processing described above — after the parent accepts the linked account terms and privacy policy as described above. Dial Santa does not show third-party ads. Advertising measurement is accepted by the parent at sign-up, happens once after the parent's email is verified, can be turned off under Parents, and never receives child profile or call data. We follow the principles of COPPA: minimal collection, parental consent, no behavioral advertising to children, and parental control over deletion.

Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account: open Parents → Account → Delete account, verify your email and confirm. We remove your account email, minute balance and call records from our active database and clear the child profile and history on that device. Other signed-in devices are signed out and clear their local account data when they next contact our server. Call-provider cleanup runs automatically in the background and retries temporary failures. Cleanup usually takes a few minutes; temporary outages can take longer. Check Parents → Account for a completion confirmation, or contact support.
  • Sign out: open Parents → Account → Sign out. Purchased minutes remain with your email, while the child profile and local history are removed from that device.
  • See your data: contact support to request access.
  • Stop future call sharing: end any active call and do not start another AI call. You can sign out under Parents → Account; this prevents new online calls on that device until you sign in again. Offline Activities remain available. Build 40 has no Parents AI-sharing toggle. Stopping calls or signing out does not erase data already processed. Use account deletion or contact support for deletion requests.
  • Advertising measurement: open Parents → Privacy & safety → Turn off ad measurement. Dial Santa then shares nothing more with Meta from that device, even if you sign in again, and you stay signed in. You can also decline Apple's tracking prompt, or turn off Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, to withhold the advertising identifier. Some U.S. state privacy laws may treat sharing device information for ad measurement as "sharing" personal information; the Parents control is how you opt out on your device, and you can also email us.
  • Account deletion removes unused minutes and does not automatically issue a refund. Contact support for help with a refund.

Security & retention

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Sign-in codes are stored only as one-way hashes and expire in 10 minutes. We keep account and purchase records while your account exists and as required for tax and accounting; call-metering records are kept only as long as needed for billing accuracy. After account deletion, we retain keyed account digests and payment references to prevent old credentials or duplicate payment notifications from restoring an erased account. Payment processors may retain transaction records. Temporary provider-erasure jobs retain the references needed to complete downstream deletion; they are removed after completion. Provider cleanup follows the limits explained above; separate technical, security or legal records may remain under provider terms.
