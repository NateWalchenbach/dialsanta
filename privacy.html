PRIVACY POLICY

Your family's privacy, plainly.

Effective September 18, 2026
The short version: we collect a parent's email address and the small details you choose to share so Santa can personalize the call (a first name, a wish list). Calls happen live — we don't record or store your child's voice or video; our call provider can keep text transcripts, which deleting your account removes. We do not show third-party ads or sell data. When a parent signs up in the app, Dial Santa tells Meta once that the app was installed and opened, so we can see which of our ads work; this never includes child profiles, voice, calls, email, or purchases. Turn that off, sign out, or permanently delete your account in the app under Parents.

Who we are

Dial Santa ("we," "us") is a parent-directed entertainment app: a grown-up sets everything up and buys minutes, and a child uses it together with their parent or guardian. This policy covers the Dial Santa iOS app and this website.

What we collect

  • Parent email address. This is your account. We use it to sign you in (a one-time code, no password), attach your purchased minutes, and send purchase receipts. Joining the website launch waitlist is a separate, optional choice.
  • Call personalization you provide. A child's first name, and optionally an age, city, wish list, or good deeds — whatever a parent chooses to enter. These exist so Santa can greet your child and hold a real conversation.
  • Purchase records. Which minute pack you bought and when. Payments themselves are handled entirely by Stripe — we never see or store card numbers.
  • Basic call records. When a call happened and how long it lasted, so we can meter minutes correctly and refund calls that fail.

Launch waitlist

The website waitlist is for adults. If you join, we save your email address, signup date, and the consent you gave so we can send you one email when Dial Santa launches. Joining does not create an app account or purchase anything. We do not add app account emails to the waitlist automatically or use the list for unrelated marketing.

Waitlist addresses are stored in our Railway database until we send the launch announcement or you ask us to remove yours. To leave the waitlist before launch, email nate.walchenbach@gmail.com. Deleting an app account also removes the matching waitlist address. We briefly store a keyed, unreadable version of the signup IP address to limit spam; it expires after one hour.

What happens during a call

Calls are live conversations with an AI character. While a call is running, your child's voice is processed in real time by our AI service providers so Santa can hear and respond. This processing is what a parent consents to during setup. The camera is used only for a small self-view on the phone's own screen — that video never leaves the device and is never transmitted or stored.

  • Dial Santa does not make call recordings. Our call provider, LiveAvatar, can retain conversation transcripts and session records. Personalized call contexts are also stored by that provider. Account deletion queues removal of those contexts and conversation events, and checks that the provider transcript is empty.
  • The child's first name and the details a parent entered are passed to the AI character at the start of each call so the conversation feels personal.
  • Santa is designed never to ask a child for personal information, and never to promise specific gifts.

Service providers

A small number of companies process data on our behalf, strictly to run the product:

  • HeyGen (LiveAvatar) and ElevenLabs — render Santa's live video and voice and process the conversation audio in real time.
  • LiveKit — carries the encrypted real-time audio/video stream.
  • Stripe — processes payments and receipts.
  • Railway — hosts our servers and database (located in the United States).
  • Meta — measures which Dial Santa ads led to app installs. Once, after a parent signs up and verifies their email, Meta's SDK in the app sends an install and app-open event with basic device information (such as device model, iOS version, language, time zone, and an app-specific anonymous ID). The SDK doesn't start again when the app is opened later. Meta receives this device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. Automatic event logging, automatic advanced matching, and Meta crash reports are turned off, and Meta's Limited Data Use setting is on. We do not send child profiles, names, ages, voice, call content, email addresses, account details, or purchases to Meta. Information Meta has already received is handled under Meta's own policies.

These providers act under their own privacy and security commitments and receive only what's needed for their job. We never sell personal information.

Children's privacy

Dial Santa is built for parents to run. Accounts, purchases, and setup are done by an adult; a parental gate protects anything involving money, privacy controls, or leaving the app. We collect from children only what a parent enters on their behalf (like a first name), plus the real-time voice processing described above — with the parent's consent, given during setup. Dial Santa does not show third-party ads. Advertising measurement is accepted by the parent at sign-up, happens once after the parent's email is verified, can be turned off under Parents, and never receives child profile or call data. We follow the principles of COPPA: minimal collection, parental consent, no behavioral advertising to children, and parental control over deletion.

Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account: open Parents → Account → Delete account, verify your email and confirm. We remove your account email, minute balance and call records from our active database and clear the child profile and history on that device. Other signed-in devices are signed out and clear their local account data when they next contact our server. Call-provider cleanup runs automatically in the background and retries temporary failures. Cleanup usually takes a few minutes; temporary outages can take longer. Check Parents → Account for a completion confirmation, or contact support.
  • Sign out: open Parents → Account → Sign out. Purchased minutes remain with your email, while the child profile and local history are removed from that device.
  • See your data: contact support to request access.
  • Withdraw consent: delete your account using the controls above, or contact support.
  • Advertising measurement: open Parents → Privacy & safety → Turn off ad measurement. Dial Santa then shares nothing more with Meta from that device, even if you sign in again, and you stay signed in. You can also decline Apple's tracking prompt, or turn off Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, to withhold the advertising identifier. Some U.S. state privacy laws may treat sharing device information for ad measurement as "sharing" personal information; the Parents control is how you opt out on your device, and you can also email us.
  • Account deletion removes unused minutes and does not automatically issue a refund. Contact support for help with a refund.

Security & retention

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Sign-in codes are stored only as one-way hashes and expire in 10 minutes. We keep account and purchase records while your account exists and as required for tax and accounting; call-metering records are kept only as long as needed for billing accuracy. After account deletion, we retain keyed account digests and payment references to prevent old credentials or duplicate payment notifications from restoring an erased account. Payment processors may retain transaction records. Temporary provider-erasure jobs retain the references needed to complete downstream deletion; they are removed after completion. Call transcripts and personalized call contexts kept by our call provider are removed when you delete your account.
