1. What Dial Santa is

Dial Santa is an entertainment app that lets families have live, personalized video calls with holiday characters. The characters are AI-generated performances — a live video avatar with an AI voice holding a real-time conversation. It's theater, made with care. 3. Who can use it

Accounts, purchases, and setup are for adults — you must be 18 or older and the parent or legal guardian of any child who joins a call. Children use Dial Santa under your supervision. You're responsible for the information you enter and for activity on your account.

4. Minutes & payment

  • Calls are paid for with minute packs — one-time purchases, processed by Apple in the app or Stripe on the website. No subscription, no recurring charges, no free trial.
  • Minutes never expire and work with every available character. They're tied to your account (your email), aren't transferable, and have no cash value beyond our refund policy below.
  • Metering is per second of connected call time. If a character fails to answer or a call fails to connect, those minutes are automatically returned — you only pay for conversation that actually happened.
  • Prices are shown at purchase and can change for future purchases; minutes you already own are never affected.

5. Refunds

For in-app purchases processed by Apple, request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem service; Apple handles those refunds. For website purchases processed by Stripe, email us within 30 days of purchase and we’ll refund unused minutes to your original payment method. Contact us if a call failed or you need help.

6. The AI, honestly

  • Conversations are generated live by AI. They're designed to be warm, safe, and age-appropriate, but like any live performance they can occasionally be imperfect or say something odd. A parent should stay nearby during calls.
  • Characters never ask for personal information and are instructed never to promise specific gifts — please don't rely on anything said in a call as a commitment by anyone.
  • Dial Santa is not affiliated with any other Santa-related product, brand, or (to our knowledge) the North Pole.

You can turn measurement off anytime in the app under Parents → Privacy & safety; that choice stays on the device even if you sign in again.
